# Phylogenetic geometry, in Lean

[![CI](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylo-geometry/actions/workflows/ci.yml/badge.svg)](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylo-geometry/actions/workflows/ci.yml)

Lean 4 formalizations of papers on spaces of phylogenetic trees and networks: their metric geometry
and geodesics, statistics and optimization in tree space, and tropical and information geometry.

This repository is a hub of theory in the collection rooted at
[lean4-phylogenetics](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics). It holds the library
[`PhyloGeometry`](PhyloGeometry), for what two or more of its papers share and the root library does
not hold. It has no modules yet; its [README](PhyloGeometry/README.md) says what goes there, and the
catalog of the papers, [`catalog/papers.toml`](catalog/papers.toml), from which the tables below are
generated.

The collection's
[conventions](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics/blob/main/docs/CONVENTIONS.md) and
[workflow](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics/blob/main/docs/WORKFLOW.md) are in the
root. Each paper is formalized with the
[formalize-math-paper](https://github.com/vltanh/formalize-math-paper) skill: its results are proved
by the paper's own arguments, and an audit reports its errors and gaps.

## The papers

Each paper is in the family of its question. The discrete characterization of trees is in
[lean4-phylo-combinatorics](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylo-combinatorics), invariants in
[lean4-phylo-algebraic](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylo-algebraic), and consistency in
[lean4-phylo-statistics](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylo-statistics). *Uses* lists the papers,
of any hub, whose results a paper's proofs are known to use, and the other hubs whose library it
requires.

## Starting a paper

From [lean4-phylogenetics](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics), when the paper's
formalization starts:

```sh
python3 scripts/status.py next              # the open papers, by priority
python3 scripts/new_paper.py <id>           # creates ../papers/geometry/lean4-<id>
cd ../papers/geometry/lean4-<id> && claude  # then: /formalize-math-paper <the paper's link>
```

## Building

```sh
lake exe cache get
lake build
lake build Phylogenetics
lake env lean scripts/Audit.lean
```

Lean and Mathlib `v4.37.0-rc1`. [`lakefile.toml`](lakefile.toml) requires lean4-phylogenetics at the
commits of the train. The audit, [`scripts/Audit.lean`](scripts/Audit.lean), checks that every
declaration of this library and of the libraries it requires uses only Lean's standard axioms;
`python3 scripts/gen_audit.py` writes its `import all` lines. CI builds the library, runs the audit,
and checks that the tables above are current.
